# Horizon Core — Pricing & Commercial Model

## The License IS the Architecture
You don't buy software and manage licenses separately. The license is committed on-chain, ratified by the Byzantine quorum, and enforced by consensus nodes at commit time.

## Pricing Tiers
| Tier | Licensed Volume | Federated Nodes | Target Client | Annual License |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pilot** | 100 TPS | 0 | POC / evaluation | $15K (90-day) |
| **Starter** | 500 TPS | 1 node | Regional bank | $75K / year |
| **Business** | 1,500 TPS | 1 node | National bank | $175K / year |
| **Enterprise** | 4,000 TPS | 2 nodes | Tier-1 bank | $350K / year |
| **Consortium** | Unlimited | 3+ nodes | Founding members | $500K / year |

## Add-On Services
- **Federated node deployment:** $25K / node (One-time)
- **Implementation & integration:** $50K–$150K
- **Annual support:** 20% of license
- **Custom security audit:** $40K

## TCO Example (National Bank, 3 Years)
- Year 1: $335K (License + Node + Implementation + Support)
- Year 2: $210K
- Year 3: $210K
